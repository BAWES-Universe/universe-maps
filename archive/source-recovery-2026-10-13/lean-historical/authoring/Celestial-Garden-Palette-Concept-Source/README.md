Concept only. Existing expanded r4 playable runtime is unchanged.

Direction: deep indigo/lavender sky, silver-blue waterfalls, quieter foliage and flowers, soft warm cream paving and gold portal light. This concentrates attention on the traversable garden and five destinations rather than competing electric-blue background light.
