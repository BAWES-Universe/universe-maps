# N24: Basalt Geology Field Camp

Outdoor volcanic specimen camp with paired shelter desks, rock-comparison stations and glacial-river lookout.

Open map32/map.tmj with adjacent atlases; map.wam has named, authorable areas without connected services. Workstations and setting objects are illustrative. Native32 Woka/world scale; unchanged standing frames and ordinary click-to-walk. No new engine code, automatic facing or sitting animation.

Rebuild using sh tools/rebuild.sh with Python3/Pillow/NumPy/ReportLab, Node24, pdftoppm. Frozen generated art, semantic chair alpha ownership, pinned native fixtures and varied repository actor provenance included. Runtime is byte-exact reproducible; PDF metadata can vary.

Author checks: every 215 admitted floor cell has native forward/return routing, exact endpoint assertions and <=0.5px interpolated body checks. All actual TMJ spawn extrema reach every seat exactly. Semantic chair rails have zero lost pixels and no head intersections. Occupied proof is decoded from TMJ layers with actual arrival coordinates/facings; manually faced proof is separately labelled.

Factual lessons:
- N23 showed that natural broad circulation can avoid destructive access revisions. N24 keeps a single-plane working plateau and broad field trail inside visibly cliff-bounded terrain.
- Generated a distinct khaki canvas folding-chair asset for outdoor research; desk pairs and actual rear-footprint tiles are authored separately rather than importing indoor seating offsets.
- Independent QA found that a rectangular table-front foreground candidate also retained gravel between its legs. r2 removes both table-front rectangles; only the true generated chair-rail alpha belongs above avatars in this open-air camp.
- Independent QA found small visible boulders could disappear from collision under area-threshold rasterization. r3 explicitly protects their occupied native tiles, including lookout rocks and arrival-trail stone; small props require dedicated cell checks.
- The conservative r3 rock-cell repair overblocked the clear overlook. r4 removes those three small rocks and one arrival-trail stone through a targeted art edit, preserving the cliff rim and reopening the matching clear floor rather than hiding unreachable pockets.

Basalt escarpments, boulders, sleeping/supply tents, glacier water, sample plinths and storage crates are solid scenic fixtures. Shelter desk approaches, main camp plateau, lookout and broad arrival trail are traversable.
