# N25: Ochre Archaeology Academy

Terracotta artifact-drawing school and restoration portico surrounding a protected excavated mosaic court.

Open map32/map.tmj with adjacent atlases; map.wam has named, authorable areas without connected services. Workstations and setting objects are illustrative. Native32 Woka/world scale; unchanged standing frames and ordinary click-to-walk. No new engine code, automatic facing or sitting animation.

Rebuild using sh tools/rebuild.sh with Python3/Pillow/NumPy/ReportLab, Node24, pdftoppm. Frozen generated art, semantic chair alpha ownership, pinned native fixtures and varied repository actor provenance included. Runtime is byte-exact reproducible; PDF metadata can vary.

Author checks: every 215 admitted floor cell has native forward/return routing, exact endpoint assertions and <=0.5px interpolated body checks. All actual TMJ spawn extrema reach every seat exactly. Semantic chair rails have zero lost pixels and no head intersections. Occupied proof is decoded from TMJ layers with actual arrival coordinates/facings; manually faced proof is separately labelled.

Factual lessons:
- N24 showed that tiny scattered props must be checked after rasterization and inaccessible visible floor must be solved in art. N25 uses a clearly fenced protected excavation instead of pretending its archaeological surfaces are public circulation.
- The original small classroom desks left inadequate chair depth; a targeted art edit moved and shortened those desks, leaving a real native-scale approach before the south wall.
- Closed the generated excavation-rail gap in artwork so its protected collision boundary is visually honest.
- Separate slightly off-centre desk approaches preserve two whole native floor cells for the central classroom doorway; rear chair feet do not form a continuous door-blocking strip.

Central excavation is a visibly continuous fenced protected exhibit, not a walkable digging mechanic. Ruins beyond academy walls, storage annex and desert canyon are scenic context. Classroom, viewing ring, portico and broad sandstone arrival are traversable.
