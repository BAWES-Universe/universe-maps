## Experience

A cream limestone waterwheel mill has an asymmetric cobbled work yard, two shallow marbling trays, a broad drying bridge over the millrace, and a right-bank rack. The actual mill door enters its separately navigable pattern cabinet room and returns through the same explicit doorway. Two original low beech paper-maker stools have individually measured native32 contacts and real return routes.

Choose Swirl at the west tray, Rake at the east tray, and Comb in the pattern room. Each optional pass may be used once per sheet. The chosen combination deliberately determines the pattern, regardless of order: circular eyes, horizontal rake bands, upright comb arches, and original combined designs. Eight original generated sheets are visible on both trays and the interior viewing table.

Cross the bridge to explicitly lift and hang the current nonblank sheet on the drying rack. A hanging marbled print completes a sheet. Applying later passes or starting a fresh wet sheet preserves the previous hanging print. Only another explicit lift replaces it. Eight wet patterns times eight printed states including blank yield 64 unique physical displays. A field guide and optional completion summary describe the visual patterns in words. Proximity opens prompts but never makes a pass, lifts paper or resets anything. Close is nondestructive; reset/replay is explicit.

All progress is per visitor for this local visit. Door travel preserves it; full reload resets it. No shared multiplayer or persistent-save claim, no external services, audio, real manufacturing process or automatic sitting/facing is implemented.
