# StudentHub: one connected office, room for each team

Updated 9 October 2026 following the user's 11:04–11:06 UTC clarification, relayed by the parent task. This version supersedes the earlier four-seat-office proposal. The first PNG, SVG, source and written snapshot are preserved unchanged.

## Current decision

StudentHub needs a substantially larger interior with distinct, nearby team desk clusters. The office remains one coherent connected building. Operations, Recruitment, Sales, Tech, Design, Marketing and Research must not be collapsed into four general-purpose desks or only labeled tool counters.

Ahmed has a recognizable personal place within Operations. Fawaz has a recognizable personal place within Recruitment, next to that team's work areas. Sales and the other departments likewise have their own legible clusters. Every personal place should have its own claimable footprint. Exact headcounts are not known, so the plan uses provisional, extensible pod modules rather than assigning a fixed team size.

The user likes the magical styling but rejected the painted study's top-down scale and furniture function. This board is a top-down spatial proposal, not a claim that the earlier painted room is correctly scaled or fit for production. Correct projection, desk/chair relationships and the actual 32 px Woka remain acceptance gates before more full-scene art.

## Office arrangement

One StudentHub shell contains two banks of department pods, a shared central cross-spine, reception, an informal lounge and an attached quiet-meeting gallery.

North bank:
- Operations: Ahmed's personal footprint and neighboring team positions; operations dashboards belong within this cluster.
- Recruitment: Fawaz's personal footprint and neighboring recruitment-team positions; keep its collaboration boundary legible.
- Sales: distinct team cluster with CRM-oriented work surfaces.
- Tech: neighboring technical-team cluster with room to expand.

South bank:
- Design / Media: distinct design-team cluster with shared Imagine, music and review tools. The combined label identifies compatible tools, not a reduction of the design team to a single creator desk.
- Marketing: distinct adjacent team cluster.
- Research: researchers' own cluster, with lab/knowledge surfaces near their desks.
- Flexible growth: unallocated expandable work area. This is a reserved footprint, not an inferred additional department.

Each pod shows two example personal desk footprints plus two growth placeholders solely to explain the module. Those symbols are not claimed actual team counts, approved staffing, final capacity, or deployed ownership. Modules can add rows or extend into the growth bay once needs are established.

Personal plots are drawn as 128 × 128 px (4 × 4 tiles) illustrative footprints. Each example holds a 64 × 32 px desk, a 24 × 24 px chair, an occupant position and remaining approach space. The final dimensions must follow the verified claim-area behavior and native collision/visual tests. Furniture or personalized decorations must not spill into the shared aisle.

## Connection and focus

The shared spine is the route between pods. It allows movement and visibility without making colleagues walk through one another's desks. Low planted partitions, floor/rug changes and restrained glazing can distinguish teams while preserving the room's common roofline and sightlines. Solid full-height department walls or separate department buildings would defeat the brief.

The larger office cannot place every colleague into every player's normal camera frame. The useful goal is visible neighboring teams, a legible shared spine and continuous short routes, not shrinking the whole office or Woka to fit one screen.

Spatial buffers alone do not guarantee that one team cannot interrupt another. Team conversation boundaries, proximity behavior, meeting isolation, ownership, guest access and claim permissions require real-client configuration and tests. Do not promise private audio because a rug, planter or glass panel is shown.

## Reception and meeting gallery

The reception counter sits beside the central approach, leaving the main path open. A team/visitor welcome position is in front of the shared spine. The informal couch-and-carpet lounge sits beside reception, separate from work-pod footprints.

The right-hand meeting gallery remains attached to the office. It contains two quiet rooms and one flexible conference room. An internal connection serves the team. A front visitor route from the public cloister can reach the gallery without crossing department desks, subject to the eventual access configuration.

## Arrival and public campus

1. Giant gate and sheltered arrival forecourt.
2. Quiet lantern avenue with planting rather than market inventory.
3. Fountain court: the fountain is offset from the direct line to the StudentHub doorway.
4. Reception, then the shared office spine and distinct neighboring department pods.
5. Public branches reach teaching/events, the river garden and the optional back lane without crossing desks.

The left civic pavilion contains a teacher-led classroom and an event hall. The classroom has a clear teaching front, student desk orientation and rear/side entry. The great hall has a stage, podium, facing audience, center aisle and public entry. These remain real functional destinations outside the everyday work floor.

## Small market behind StudentHub

The market moves behind the office, away from the arrival-facing avenue. Two modest booth footprints illustrate the small current inventory requirement. The number and contents remain configurable; the plan does not invent stock or live services.

An obvious public side lane runs around StudentHub's west side to the back-lane market. A modest sign at the fountain/cloister branch can point to it. Visiting the market is an optional turn rather than a required arrival sequence. The side lane remains outside the office shell and does not cut through department pods.

## Riverside garden and firepit

Use one riverside, not both a river and an ocean. The eastern garden contains water-edge seating and a comfortable open firepit circle suitable for informal conversation or a conference break.

A public route runs from the fountain/commons to the riverside garden and around the firepit. It never requires entry into StudentHub or passage through desk clusters. The water edge needs a real collision boundary; seats and firepit approaches need clear standing positions. Planting frames the paths without concealing them.

Fire crackle is a proposed local sound source. It should be audible close to the seating circle, fade gently outside the hangout and remain inaudible at team desks and meeting rooms. An initial spatial-audio design could use full presence inside about 5 tiles and fade to silence by about 12 tiles, subject to quiet-volume and real-device listening tests. This is not a claim audio is activated. Respect user audio/mute and reduced-motion settings, and avoid automatic large-area ambience.

## Portals and future services

A smaller portal garden is reached from the optional public side lane. Larger creator rooms, labs, event worlds and other destinations can extend the campus without scattering the daily team. Physical portals and Explore room navigation are distinct from walking between department desks.

Reserved bot, guide, shop and service positions remain future hooks. The diagram does not assert active commerce, recruitment services, account connections, private CRM access, claim ownership or live AI guides.

## Provisional geometry

The combined board shows a conceptual 100 × 112 tile campus envelope at 32 px/tile (3200 × 3584 px). It is a planning envelope, not a final rendered world size.

StudentHub shell: 1984 × 1344 px, approximately 62 × 42 tiles. This replaces the earlier 1024 px painted slice as the office capacity constraint. The single shell is at concept world origin (512, 320).

Pod modules: 352 × 384 px, about 11 × 12 tiles, with varying team capacities still to be established. Main pod cross-spine: 256 px (8 tiles). Central reception approach: 128 px (4 tiles). Side circulation and public routes: normally at least 96 px (3 tiles). Desk and Woka scale are not enlarged to fill a scene.

The module grid is illustrative. It must be refined after verifying the claim contract and actual desk/chair assets. Do not port the earlier four-seat painting's coordinates or the superseded six-seat alternative into this larger plan.

## Roof and glass states

Keep one coherent StudentHub roofline, entrance, wall perimeter and shared architectural identity. Interior reveal should hide/fade only obstructing roof surfaces. Retain perimeter rim, wall tops, columns, glass frames and low foreground boundaries so the larger room remains legible.

Roof reveal and acoustic/claim regions must not change permanent physical collision. Nearby teams should remain visible through the open plan or restrained glazing. A glass meeting wall should keep the meeting table, occupants and approach visible while privacy is enforced by actual runtime configuration.

- Verify roof/glass state transitions retain building shape and do not alter collision.
