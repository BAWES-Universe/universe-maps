# Archive status: connected glass proof

The owner rejected this proof as the main Universe art direction on 2026-10-09: it still looks flat and pasted, with inadequate lighting and contact shadows. Preserve it for learning and iteration, not as a ready main office.

The original frozen map also contains `silent: true` on `team-inside`. That disables proximity conversations, not just ambient sound, and is inappropriate for the intended shared workplace. Do not advertise this frozen version as a ready conversational office. Its original code and delivered files remain preserved.

The separate roofless future template removes the roof, reveal script and generic silent property while retaining the visual layers and collision footprint. That derivative is the starting point for any future outdoor office room. Quiet ambience must never be implemented by disabling people's conversations.
