# Visual asset provenance

## Selected WA Village atlases

Source: https://github.com/workadventure/wa-village/tree/master/tilesets . The referenced map identifies 100 Roads Inc. as owner and reserves reproduction/distribution/derivative rights. The user explicitly reported permission through the WorkAdventure partnership. These files are used on that project-specific authorization; no general or open-source license is asserted.

Retained originals in `licensed-wa-village/`: tables, chairs, couches, 100R_Floor, decor, trees-shadow and grounds-assets. `mini-trees.png` was inspected but not used because its artwork contains snow. No full reference map layer or building layout is copied. The original campus compiler selects furniture, material and planting rectangles, places them on independently authored coordinates, and maintains separate explicit collision and character-overlap layers. `asset-manifest.json` records original byte hashes and source URLs.

## BAWES architecture

`tower-polished-original.png` is a built-in image-tool redraw of the earlier original BAWES tower, informed by the reference's architectural depth. It adds structural side planes, warm stone piers, deep eaves and planted terraces. `tower-complete-original.png` is the final edit adding stepped occupied wings and garden terraces. `portal-pavilion-polished-original.png` supplies the curved conservatory shell; `portal-polished-original.png` supplies the individual open portal arches. `cowork-polished-original.png` is an original generated sibling building, with a subsequent alpha-opening correction. The generation originals remain in `/workspace/generated_images` in the saved environment. No external paid service was used.

The compiler fits the foreground podium in sections so its alpha entrance is exactly aligned with the authored six-tile door. The entire projected building footprint remains usable beneath the removable shell. This fitting changes artwork placement, never Woka scale or camera scale. Native gameplay screenshots, rather than the generation previews, determine acceptance.

## Compiler-authored elements

Street layout, plaza inlays, rugs, shoreline, water animation, interaction controls, collision, routes and area coordinates remain compiler-authored. User-facing text is not baked into artwork. Source metadata and this provenance file are technical documentation, not in-world labels.
