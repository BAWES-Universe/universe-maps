- `whole-campus-concept.png`: 1536×1024, despite a requested 4608×3072. Complete composition with the existing broad island arrangement; not native-resolution map coverage.
- `cafe-whole-region-concept.png`: 1281×1228. Complete café island, bar, paired lounge groups, pond, waterfall and bridge approach. Furniture scale and bridge registration do not match the playable map.
- `conservatory-whole-region-concept.png`: 1096×1435. Complete conservatory island and architectural lounge. Recessed entry, scale and bridge registration remain unresolved.

The generation used `current-layout-preview.png` plus the retained approved campus concept for the whole scene; the region-reference PNGs are crops of that new full-scene concept. Regional prompts requested consistent illustrated stone, planting, glass, gold trim and daylight, a 32px native Woka context, 144px sofas, 64px tables and clear circulation. The outputs did not reliably satisfy those scale constraints. They are visual alternatives, not geometry-authoritative sources.

Do not enlarge the full-scene output or paste isolated crisp furniture onto the softened original scenery. New corrections must cover complete registered regions and prove shared seams at native gameplay scale.
