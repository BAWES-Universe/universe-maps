The safe setup guide leads with native editing and warns against destructive map URL resubmission or blind ZIP replacement.

The foyer's low planted bay edges prevent the tested automatic paths from cutting through other meetings. This is finite route coverage, not a proof about every arbitrary click, asynchronous media transition or participant choice. The terrace garden is a closed overlook; it is not an extra playable garden map. There is no purchasing flow or ambient music.
