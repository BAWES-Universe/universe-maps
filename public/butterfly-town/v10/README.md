# Lush Campus10: restored24 seats, wider doors and living water

This separate native32 feedback map preserves Campus09 and the existing gate versions. It widens and aligns visible door approaches, restores substantial fixed chair-back occlusion, and retains the requested auditorium layout: two banks of three chairs across four rows,24 seats in total. The six-seat classroom, eight ordinary workplace seats, two reception positions, central carpet and speaker podium remain.

A continuous exterior pass replaces the duplicated tree strips, closes structural alpha gaps, aligns the market landing to the shared paving and preserves the fountain/fire garden. Native Tiled animation now includes fountain jets and ripples, broad river flow, fire variation, and two small garden insects/light accents. Spawn has native proximity silence; fountain/fire ambience remains governed by normal game audio controls.

Use map32/magical-campus.tmj as the custom TMJ. It intentionally references hash-pinned preserved v9 images/audio at relative ../../v9/map32/ paths; keep those shared assets in place. The hosted map is a pixel/frame/collision-equivalent derivative of the separately saved complete standalone bundle. No engine patch or scripting controller is required. The stage companion must be loaded through supported map-storage/WAM import or recreated in the native area editor when its broadcast-area feature is enabled; do not replace an existing room WAM with this minimal fresh-room file. Gate entry is configured separately by the room owner.

The checks in docs/VALIDATION-SUMMARY.json are source/native simulations and decoded-raster verification, not live-room or physical-phone acceptance. Native global tap routing ignores players; other avatars are pass-through, so dense-row travel may visually cross occupants; all24 seats are reachable under native map collision. Chair positions do not snap or auto-turn avatars into a seated pose. Roofless interiors remain deliberate in this feedback preview. Two tiny existing fountain-canopy brushes remain documented.

Normal native mute/pause/block-audio applies. Audio is bounded rectangular gain bands with preattenuated files; unsupported-volume devices receive a fixed safe level, not continuous spatial mixing. Spawn's initial socket-group race is tracked at https://github.com/BAWES-Universe/workadventure-universe/issues/735 . The optional map32/magical-campus.wam and docs/STAGE-SETUP.md provide the native podium/audience configuration for a fresh room or careful editor setup. A TMJ import alone does not activate broadcasting, and no live connection test is claimed. Other live bots, claim ownership and meeting services remain unconfigured.

The small publication payload reuses preserved v9 atlas tiles with identical RGBA, instead of duplicating them. Decoded texture storage is approximately49.3 MB versus43.1 MB for the independent packing; both have four tile layers and30,176 tile objects. Future device feedback should judge this tradeoff.

The editor Podium/Audience buttons depend on FEATURE_FLAG_BROADCAST_AREAS (source default false; deployed value unverified). The companion is a schema-valid setup template, not proof that those editor controls are available or that a live stage connection is configured.
