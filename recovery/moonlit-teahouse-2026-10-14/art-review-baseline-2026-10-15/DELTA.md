# Exact delta from original N41

This is a useful rollback/restoration baseline, not a completed or newly illustrated teahouse.

Unchanged: all original artwork bytes, original tile/object layers, collision, foreground, spawn, map areas and native dimensions. The original N41 runtime files are also retained byte-for-byte. No roof, door, glass, furniture or interior artwork was added. No water animation was added.

New implementation: separate moonlit-review.tmj with a script property and transparent full-map native-audio tile layer; scene.js adapts optional overview/automatic return, movement cancellation and explicit bell audio enable/silence from r4. Full r4 controller preserved separately. Actual local gameplay screenshots and route/control observations are new.

Teahouse progress: restores an appropriate visual foundation and keeps useful controls, but does not solve the required teahouse assembly. The existing gazebo is a shrine containing a statue. Missing assets are a matching separable roof, exposed furnished interior, glass/frame treatment, animated doorway and counter/table surfaces. These were not replaced by placeholder geometry.

Visual caveat: fractional overview zoom has thin tile seams. Normal walking views are the primary comparison. No final visual approval is claimed.

Library upload was attempted and retried once; both failed before saving files. This Git backup is not a publication or deployment.
